# Internationalization

The spike is in **prepare phase**. English must be complete and visible
frontplane strings must be centralized. Missing non-English catalogs are
permitted until the UI stabilizes.

The infrastructure must be able to recognize the canonical locale set:

~~~text
am ar az bg bn bs da de el en es fa fi fil fr ha he hi hr hu id ig is it ja
km ko mk nb nl pa pl ps pt_br ro ru sl sq sr sv sw ta th tr uk ur vi yo
zh_hans zh_hant
~~~

RTL locales are ar, he, fa, ps, and ur. No translation corpus or localized
command aliases are part of this feasibility spike.

The WAT ABI carries stable numeric action IDs and UTF-8 labels; locale handling
is a host responsibility and does not change application logic.
